# 👋 Welcome to Whollify

**Education was built around teaching. We built Whollify around learning.**

Whollify is the digital infrastructure for learning, built around the people actually doing it. Instead of stitching together a video tool, a chat app, a gradebook, an LMS and three spreadsheets, everything lives in one place and shares one record.

[**whollify.com**](https://whollify.com)

---

## 🎯 Learning is not a spectator sport

Most platforms put an instructor at the front and everyone else in the audience. We inverted that.

On Whollify, **learners evaluate each other**, and evaluation is a first-class part of the product rather than an afterthought. Every review is attributed, so evaluators are named and are themselves graded independently by AI. Feedback quality becomes visible, accountable, and part of your record.

A teacher here is an overseer rather than a lecturer. The platform supplies the structure, the material, the evaluation machinery and the social surface. The learning happens between people.

---

## 🧩 One product, many schools

We used to ship two experiences: one for individuals, one for institutions. We do not any more.

There is a school, and you are in one. Sign up as an individual and you join our house school. Bring a university, department, bootcamp or training organisation and you get your own. Same codebase, same interface, same features.

What differs between any two people is only:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Which school** they belong to |
| **What role** they hold in it |
| **Which apps** that school has enabled |
| **What permissions** they have |

## ⚙️ Under the hood

Submissions are executed and assessed automatically inside sandboxed, containerised runners with resource and time limits, so untrusted code never touches anything it should not. Exercise definitions are language-agnostic, which means one problem specification can drive tracks in several languages at once.
